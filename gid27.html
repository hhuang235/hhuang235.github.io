About the workshop

Geometry in Depth is a workshop series that gives graduate students and researchers in related areas the opportunity to go beyond what one normally learns from a regular conference.

Invited speakers present two-part lectures, first giving an introduction to their research and then following up with a more in-depth lecture, with plenty of discussion afterwards.

Format

There will be four two-part talks, with each talk totaling 80 minutes (40 minutes for each part). The first part will establish context for the research-focused second part. The workshop will also include an open-problems session and ample time for discussion.
