Geometry in Depth 2027

Texas A&M University · January 23–24, 2027

About the conference

Geometry in Depth is a workshop series that gives graduate students and researchers in related areas the opportunity to go beyond what one normally learns from a regular conference. Invited speakers present two-part lectures, first giving an introduction to their research and then following up with a more in-depth lecture, with plenty of discussion afterwards.

Format: There will be four two-part talks, with each talk totaling 80 minutes (40 minutes for each part). The first part will be introductory and will establish context for the research-focused second part. In addition to the lectures, there will be a session devoted to open problems, with time throughout the workshop for discussion.
